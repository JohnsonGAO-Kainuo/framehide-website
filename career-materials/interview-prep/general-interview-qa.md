# General Interview Introduction and Q&A

This is the reusable baseline for Johnson's interviews in international trade, business development, operations and project-support roles. It is not a script to recite word for word. Keep the facts stable, then adjust the final 20% to the company and job description.

## 1. Core Positioning

```text
An early-career project and operations professional who has supported cross-functional healthcare technology projects and independently tested client delivery, digital-product promotion and an overseas B2B trading workflow.
```

中文理解：

```text
具备跨部门项目协调经验，并亲自完成过客户交付、数字产品推广和海外 B2B 外贸流程验证的早期职业阶段项目与运营人才。
```

Do not position yourself as a senior manager, software engineer or established international trader. Your advantage is hands-on execution across several business functions.

## 2. Self-Introduction

### 30-second English version

```text
Hello, I’m Johnson Gao. I hold a master’s degree in Programme and Project Management from the University of Warwick. My experience covers cross-functional project coordination, data reporting, client delivery and early-stage B2B trade. I previously supported multiple healthcare technology projects, and more recently built and tested website, digital-product and camera-accessories trading workflows through my own company. I’m now looking to bring this hands-on experience into an international trade, business development or operations team where I can contribute and continue developing professionally.
```

### Standard 75-90 second English version

```text
Hello, I’m Johnson Gao. I hold a master’s degree in Programme and Project Management from the University of Warwick, as well as bachelor’s degrees in Business Management and Applied Psychology from Hong Kong Metropolitan University.

I previously worked in the project management team at the Hong Kong Centre for Cerebro-Cardiovascular Health Engineering. I supported multiple healthcare technology projects by coordinating management, project teams and software, hardware, AI and finance-related support functions. I also organised meetings, tracked action items and helped turn clinical research and community programme data into Excel and Tableau dashboards.

After that, I established a small company to test end-to-end business execution. I delivered two client websites, launched four digital products, tested promotion through SEO, Google Ads, social media and Product Hunt, and built FrameHide’s B2B export workflow from supplier sourcing and retailer research to cold outreach, quotation and sample logistics. This progressed an Australian camera retailer from initial contact to receiving a product evaluation sample.

These experiences strengthened my project coordination, commercial awareness and ability to execute with limited resources. I’m now looking to apply those skills within a structured international trade, business development or operations team and continue developing through larger-scale work and collaboration.
```

### 中文参考

```text
您好，我是 Johnson Gao。我在华威大学完成了 Programme and Project Management 硕士课程，本科则在香港都会大学修读商业管理和应用心理学双学位。

我之前在香港心脑血管健康工程研究中心的项目管理团队担任项目助理，负责协助协调多个医疗科技项目，对接管理层、项目团队以及软件、硬件、AI 和财务相关支持部门。我也会组织会议、跟进行动事项，并将临床研究和社区项目数据整理成 Excel 及 Tableau 可视化看板。

之后我成立了一家小型公司，尝试独立跑通从规划到交付的业务流程。我完成了两个客户网站，推出四款数字产品，通过 SEO、Google Ads、社交媒体和 Product Hunt 测试产品推广，也搭建了 FrameHide 的 B2B 外贸流程，包括供应商寻找、海外零售商调研、开发信、报价和样品物流，并将一名澳大利亚相机零售商推进至收到产品评估样品。

这些经历提升了我的项目协调、商业意识和在有限资源下推进工作的能力。我现在希望将这些能力带入一个更有结构的外贸、业务开发或运营团队，在真实业务中继续学习并作出贡献。
```

## 3. Core Interview Q&A

### Q1. Why did you start your own company?

```text
I wanted to test whether I could take an idea from planning to actual delivery rather than only supporting one part of a process. I therefore established a small company and worked on client websites, digital-product validation and an early-stage B2B trading project. The experience gave me direct exposure to customer requirements, proposals, supplier communication, promotion, logistics and follow-up. It also helped me understand both the value and the limitations of working alone.
```

中文思路：强调主动验证完整业务流程，不要说因为无法适应职场，也不要批评上一家公司。

### Q2. Why are you returning to full-time employment?

```text
The entrepreneurial period helped me build strong ownership and hands-on execution, but it also clarified that I want to develop within a team that has established customers, experienced colleagues and larger projects. I would like to contribute what I have learned while gaining deeper industry knowledge and more systematic commercial experience. I am not treating employment as a temporary fallback; I am looking for a role where I can contribute consistently and continue growing.
```

中文思路：不要只说缺钱，也不要说以后随时会再次离职。核心是希望进入更成熟的团队、接触更大项目和系统化业务。

### Q3. Was the business unsuccessful?

```text
It completed several early validation milestones but had not yet reached stable, scalable revenue. I delivered two paid website projects, launched products across web, desktop and iOS, gained initial users and paid subscribers, and completed an overseas retailer sample evaluation workflow. I therefore would not present it as a scaled success, but it was a valuable and measurable operating experience that taught me how to validate demand, control costs and respond to market feedback.
```

中文思路：承认尚未规模化，不把它包装成成功创业，也不要用“完全失败”否定已有成果。

### Q4. Will your company affect your full-time work?

```text
No. If I join a company, my full-time responsibilities will take priority. I understand the importance of working hours, confidentiality and conflict-of-interest requirements, and I will comply with the employer’s policies. Any necessary administrative matters relating to my company would be limited or handed over so that they do not affect my performance.
```

Only use this answer if you are genuinely prepared to make the full-time role the priority and comply with the employment contract.

### Q5. Why did you leave your Project Assistant role after about six months?

```text
The role gave me useful exposure to cross-functional project coordination, management reporting and healthcare technology projects. I chose to leave because I wanted to test whether I could independently take responsibility for planning, customer communication and delivery. That experience was valuable, and it has now helped me clarify that my next step should combine hands-on ownership with the structure, collaboration and learning opportunities of an established team.
```

中文思路：不主动谈试用期延长、内部冲突或工作环境。回答离职动机、学到什么以及为什么现在重新求职。

### Q6. You do not have a traditional export-sales background. Why international trade or business development?

```text
My formal title was not in export sales, but I have already completed many of the underlying tasks. For FrameHide, I researched overseas retailers, selected and communicated with suppliers, prepared product positioning and multilingual content, wrote personalised cold emails, discussed sample terms and coordinated international logistics. An Australian retailer responded and received a sample for evaluation. This experience confirmed my interest in customer development and cross-border business, and I now want to build that capability more systematically in a professional team.
```

中文思路：不要声称自己已经是成熟外贸业务员；强调已跑通过哪些环节，以及希望系统化成长。

### Q7. Tell me about an overseas customer-development example.

```text
I needed to validate whether independent camera retailers would consider a new accessory supplier, but I had a limited budget and only an early product line. I researched Sydney camera stores, removed unsuitable official or highly standardised retailers, and prioritised independent stores serving camera enthusiasts. I then prepared concise, personalised outreach with a product page and a low-risk sample offer. One retailer with experience selling established accessory brands requested a brown half case with a matching strap. I confirmed the delivery details, coordinated the supplier and forwarder, and delivered the sample for evaluation. It was not yet a wholesale order, but it proved that the outreach-to-sample workflow could generate genuine retailer interest.
```

Key evidence boundary: say `retailer interest`, `sample request` and `delivered evaluation sample`; do not say `partnership`, `purchase order` or `closed sale` unless these occur later.

### Q8. Tell me about coordinating multiple teams or projects.

```text
At the healthcare engineering centre, the project management team sat between management, several project teams and support functions such as software, hardware, AI and finance. Updates could come from different people and at different levels of detail. I supported the process by organising meetings, preparing agendas and minutes, recording owners and deadlines, and following up outstanding actions. I then consolidated technical and operational updates into a clearer management-facing format. This helped create a more structured record of progress, dependencies and next steps across different project workstreams.
```

中文思路：重点是你如何让信息、负责人和截止时间变得清晰，不要虚构项目节省了多少时间或提升了多少百分比。

### Q9. Tell me about your data-reporting experience.

```text
I coordinated with clinical research and community programme teams to collect and organise project data. I used Excel functions and PivotTables to clean and structure the information, then built PivotCharts and Tableau dashboards with filters so that stakeholders could review the data from different perspectives. My role was not statistical research ownership; it was to improve data quality, presentation and management visibility.
```

中文思路：明确自己做的是数据整理、清洗和可视化，不要声称负责临床结论或高级统计建模。

### Q10. What did you learn from promoting your software products?

```text
The main lesson was that launching a product is not the same as building demand. I tested SEO, Google Ads, social media and Product Hunt exposure for several products. The early campaigns generated more than 700 website users and Kinolu’s first paid subscribers, but the results also showed that traffic alone did not guarantee retention or scalable revenue. I learned to look more closely at positioning, channel quality, conversion and follow-up, and this influenced my later decision to use direct B2B outreach for FrameHide rather than relying only on passive website traffic.
```

中文思路：这个回答既证明你做过推广，也能坦诚解释为什么产品没有形成稳定收入。

### Q11. Tell me about a setback or failure.

```text
One setback was spending too much time building and polishing digital products before validating whether I had a repeatable acquisition channel. Although the products launched and attracted initial users, commercial traction remained limited. I responded by narrowing the scope of later tests, setting clearer validation milestones and speaking directly with potential customers earlier. With FrameHide, for example, I prioritised retailer outreach and sample feedback instead of waiting for website traffic alone.
```

Avoid saying that all previous work was useless. The point is that you changed your method after receiving evidence.

### Q12. What is your main strength?

```text
My main strength is turning an unclear task into a structured sequence of actions. I am comfortable gathering information, identifying the next step, coordinating different people and following through to delivery. This was useful both in the project management team, where I tracked actions across multiple workstreams, and in my own company, where I independently moved client and B2B projects from initial research to delivery or sample evaluation.
```

### Q13. What is a weakness you are working on?

```text
Earlier, I sometimes spent too long improving details before getting external feedback. Through my product and trading projects, I learned to time-box preparation, define a minimum test and use customer responses to decide what deserves further work. I still care about quality, but I now try to balance quality with speed and evidence.
```

This is more credible than using a disguised strength such as “I work too hard”.

### Q14. Why this company and this role?

Use this structure and replace every bracket before the interview:

```text
I am interested in this role for three reasons. First, the responsibilities in [customer development / trade operations / project coordination] match the work I have already started doing. Second, [specific company product, market or recent development] gives me a clear reason to join this company rather than applying only because of the job title. Third, the role would allow me to contribute my experience in [two relevant capabilities] while developing deeper knowledge in [one area from the job description].
```

Never use a completely generic answer. Research the company before every interview.

### Q15. Where do you see yourself in three to five years?

```text
I want to become a dependable commercial and operations professional who can manage customer or project workflows with increasing independence. In the near term, my priority is to build strong industry knowledge, understand the company’s products and customers, and deliver consistently. Over time, I would like to take ownership of larger accounts, markets or cross-functional projects based on performance.
```

Do not mention plans to leave and restart the business.

### Q16. What are your salary expectations?

```text
I would first like to understand the full responsibilities and the company’s range for the role. Based on the position and my previous experience, I am looking for a market-aligned package, but I am flexible depending on the scope, development opportunity and total benefits. May I ask what range has been budgeted for this position?
```

For application forms that require a number, set a role-specific range after checking comparable vacancies. Do not use one fixed number for every job.

### Q17. What is your availability?

```text
I am currently managing my own small business activities, so I do not have a conventional notice period. I can agree a practical start date and complete any necessary handover promptly.
```

If you can genuinely start immediately, replace the second sentence with: `I am available to start immediately.`

### Q18. Do you require visa sponsorship?

```text
I currently hold a valid IANG visa and can take up or change employment in Hong Kong without employer sponsorship or prior change-of-employer approval. I expect to become eligible to apply for permanent residency from September 2026, subject to Immigration Department assessment.
```

Do not state that permanent residency is guaranteed before approval.

## 4. Questions to Ask the Interviewer

Choose two or three, not all of them:

1. What would you like the person in this role to accomplish during the first three months?
2. Which markets, customer groups or product lines are currently the highest priority?
3. How are leads, quotations and customer follow-ups currently managed within the team?
4. What are the most common coordination challenges between sales, operations, suppliers and logistics?
5. How would performance in this role be measured after six months?
6. What distinguishes someone who performs well in this team from someone who is only adequate?
7. What training or support is available for learning the company’s products and industry?

## 5. Evidence and Wording Boundaries

Safe to say:

- Delivered two paid client websites.
- Launched four digital products across web, desktop and iOS.
- Tested SEO, Google Ads, social media and Product Hunt promotion.
- Generated more than 700 initial website users and Kinolu’s first paid subscribers.
- Published Mood Balloon on the Apple App Store.
- Progressed an Australian retailer from initial outreach to a delivered evaluation sample.
- Coordinated multiple healthcare technology project workstreams and built data dashboards.

Do not say unless new evidence exists:

- Built a profitable or scalable software company.
- Secured an Australian wholesale partnership or purchase order.
- Personally developed the medical AI models.
- Managed a formal team if you mainly coordinated contributors and suppliers.
- Will definitely receive Hong Kong permanent residency in September 2026.

## 6. Practice Method

1. Learn the structure, not every sentence.
2. Keep recruiter answers to 30-60 seconds and examples to 60-90 seconds.
3. Record one English practice each day and remove unnecessary background.
4. Prepare one company-specific `Why this company?` answer before every interview.
5. After each interview, record the questions, your weak answers and one change for the next interview.
