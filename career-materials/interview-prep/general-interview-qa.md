# General Interview Introduction and Q&A

This is the reusable baseline for Johnson's interviews in international trade, business development, operations and project-support roles. It is not a script to recite word for word. Keep the facts stable, then adjust the final 20% to the company and job description.

## 1. Core Positioning

```text
An early-career project and operations professional who has supported cross-functional healthcare technology projects and independently tested client delivery, digital-product promotion and an overseas B2B trading workflow.
```

中文理解：

```text
具备跨部门项目协调经验，并亲自完成过客户交付、数字产品推广和海外 B2B 外贸流程验证的早期职业阶段项目与运营人才。
```

Do not position yourself as a senior manager, software engineer or established international trader. Your advantage is hands-on execution across several business functions.

中文提醒：不要把自己定位成资深经理、软件工程师或成熟外贸业务员。你的优势是虽然仍处于职业早期，但已经实际接触项目协调、客户交付、供应商沟通、海外开发和数据汇报。

## 2. Self-Introduction

```text
Hello, I’m Johnson Gao. I hold a master’s degree in Programme and Project Management from the University of Warwick. My experience covers cross-functional project coordination, data reporting, client delivery and early-stage B2B trade. I previously supported multiple healthcare technology projects, and more recently built and tested website, digital-product and camera-accessories trading workflows through my own company. I’m now looking to bring this hands-on experience into an international trade, business development or operations team where I can contribute and continue developing professionally.
```

### Standard 75-90 second English version

```text
Hello, I’m Johnson Gao. I hold a master’s degree in Programme and Project Management from the University of Warwick, as well as bachelor’s degrees in Business Management and Applied Psychology from Hong Kong Metropolitan University.

I previously worked in the project management team at the Hong Kong Centre for Cerebro-Cardiovascular Health Engineering. ```
```

## 3. Core Interview Q&A

### Q1. Why did you start your own company?

```text
I wanted to test whether I could take an idea from planning to actual delivery rather than only supporting one part of a process. I therefore established a small company and worked on client websites, digital-product validation and an early-stage B2B trading project. The experience gave me direct exposure to customer requirements, proposals, supplier communication, promotion, logistics and follow-up. It also helped me understand both the value and the limitations of working alone.
```

中文思路：强调主动验证完整业务流程，不要说因为无法适应职场，也不要批评上一家公司。

中文参考回答：

```text
我当时希望验证自己能否不只是支持流程中的一部分，而是把一个想法从规划真正推进到交付。因此我成立了一家小型公司，尝试客户网站、数字产品验证和早期 B2B 外贸项目。这让我实际接触了客户需求、方案报价、供应商沟通、产品推广、物流和后续跟进，也让我更清楚地认识到独立工作的价值和局限。
```

### Q2. Why are you returning to full-time employment?

```text
The entrepreneurial period helped me build strong ownership and hands-on execution, but it also clarified that I want to develop within a team that has established customers, experienced colleagues and larger projects. I would like to contribute what I have learned while gaining deeper industry knowledge and more systematic commercial experience. I am not treating employment as a temporary fallback; I am looking for a role where I can contribute consistently and continue growing.
```

中文思路：不要只说缺钱，也不要说以后随时会再次离职。核心是希望进入更成熟的团队、接触更大项目和系统化业务。

中文参考回答：

```text
这段自雇经历提升了我的主人翁意识和实际执行能力，同时也让我更清楚自己希望进入一个拥有成熟客户、资深同事和更大项目的团队。我希望把已经积累的经验带进团队，同时系统学习行业和商业流程。我不是把工作当作临时退路，而是在寻找一个能够持续贡献并长期成长的岗位。
```

I delivered two paid website projects, launched products across web, desktop and iOS, gained initial users and paid subscribers, and completed an overseas retailer sample evaluation workflow. I therefore would not present it as a scaled success, but it was a valuable and measurable operating experience that taught me how to validate demand, control costs and respond to market feedback.

中文思路：承认尚未规模化，不把它包装成成功创业，也不要用“完全失败”否定已有成果。

中文参考回答：

```text
业务完成了一些早期验证，但还没有达到稳定和可规模化的收入。我完成了两个付费网站项目，上线了网页、桌面端和 iOS 产品，获得了初期用户及付费订阅，也完成了海外零售商从开发到样品评估的流程。因此我不会把它包装成已经规模化的成功，但它是一段有实际结果的运营经历，让我学会了验证需求、控制成本和根据市场反馈调整方向。
```

只有在你确实愿意把全职工作放在优先位置，并能够遵守雇佣合同时，才使用这个回答。

### Q7. Tell me about an overseas customer-development example.

```text
I needed to validate whether independent camera retailers would consider a new accessory supplier, but I had a limited budget and only an early product line. I researched Sydney camera stores, removed unsuitable official or highly standardised retailers, and prioritised independent stores serving camera enthusiasts. I then prepared concise, personalised outreach with a product page and a low-risk sample offer. One retailer with experience selling established accessory brands requested a brown half case with a matching strap. I confirmed the delivery details, coordinated the supplier and forwarder, and delivered the sample for evaluation. It was not yet a wholesale order, but it proved that the outreach-to-sample workflow could generate genuine retailer interest.
```

Key evidence boundary: say `retailer interest`, `sample request` and `delivered evaluation sample`; do not say `partnership`, `purchase order` or `closed sale` unless these occur later.

中文参考回答：

```text
为了验证独立相机零售商是否愿意考虑新的配件供应商，我先调研悉尼相机店，排除官方店和不适合的小店，优先联系服务摄影爱好者的独立零售商。我为不同店铺准备了简洁的个性化开发信、产品页面和低风险样品方案。其中一家有销售成熟配件品牌经验的零售商要求评估棕色半套和配套肩带。我确认收件资料，协调供应商和货代，并完成样品交付。它还不是批发订单，但证明了从开发信到样品评估的流程能够产生真实的零售商兴趣。
```

证据边界：目前只能说“产生兴趣”“要求样品”和“完成评估样品交付”，在付费订单出现前不能说“建立合作”“获得采购订单”或“成交”。

## 4. Questions to Ask the Interviewer

Choose two or three, not all of them:

1. What would you like the person in this role to accomplish during the first three months?
2. Which markets, customer groups or product lines are currently the highest priority?
3. How are leads, quotations and customer follow-ups currently managed within the team?
4. What are the most common coordination challenges between sales, operations, suppliers and logistics?
5. How would performance in this role be measured after six months?
6. What distinguishes someone who performs well in this team from someone who is only adequate?
7. What training or support is available for learning the company’s products and industry?

中文对照：

1. 公司希望这个岗位的人在入职前三个月完成什么？
2. 目前最优先的市场、客户群或产品线是什么？
3. 团队目前如何管理线索、报价和客户跟进？
4. 销售、运营、供应商和物流之间最常见的协调难点是什么？
5. 入职六个月后，公司会如何衡量这个岗位的表现？
6. 在这个团队中，表现优秀的人与仅仅达到要求的人有什么区别？
7. 公司会提供哪些产品或行业知识培训？

面试时选两至三个即可，不要全部提问。

## 5. Evidence and Wording Boundaries

Safe to say:

- Delivered two paid client websites.
- Launched four digital products across web, desktop and iOS.
- Tested SEO, Google Ads, social media and Product Hunt promotion.
- Generated more than 700 initial website users and Kinolu’s first paid subscribers.
- Published Mood Balloon on the Apple App Store.
- Progressed an Australian retailer from initial outreach to a delivered evaluation sample.
- Coordinated multiple healthcare technology project workstreams and built data dashboards.

Do not say unless new evidence exists:

- Built a profitable or scalable software company.
- Secured an Australian wholesale partnership or purchase order.
- Personally developed the medical AI models.
- Managed a formal team if you mainly coordinated contributors and suppliers.
- Will definitely receive Hong Kong permanent residency in September 2026.

中文证据边界：可以说已经交付两个付费网站、上线四款数字产品、测试过不同推广渠道、获得初期用户及付费订阅、完成澳洲零售商评估样品交付、协调过多个医疗科技项目并制作数据看板。不能把这些描述成可规模化盈利软件公司、澳洲批发合作或采购订单；不能声称亲自开发医疗 AI 模型或正式管理团队；永居获批前也不能说一定会在 2026 年 9 月取得永居。

## 6. Practice Method

1. Learn the structure, not every sentence.
2. Keep recruiter answers to 30-60 seconds and examples to 60-90 seconds.
3. Record one English practice each day and remove unnecessary background.
4. Prepare one company-specific `Why this company?` answer before every interview.
5. After each interview, record the questions, your weak answers and one change for the next interview.

中文练习方法：理解回答结构而不是逐字背诵；普通问题控制在 30 至 60 秒，案例控制在 60 至 90 秒；每天录制一次英文回答并删去不必要背景；每次面试前准备一个公司专属的申请理由；面试后记录问题、薄弱回答和下一次要改进的一点。
